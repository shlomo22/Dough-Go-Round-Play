Dough-Go-Round privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Dough-Go-Round is a puzzle game. This policy explains what the game does with information about you: in short, nothing leaves your device.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What the game keeps on your device

The game saves your progress (which levels you have finished and the stars you have earned), which tips you have seen, and your settings (sound, music, double speed) on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. This stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Permissions

The game asks only to use your phone's vibration, for light feedback while you play.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
